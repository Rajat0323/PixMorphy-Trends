## How to Compare AI Engineer Salaries in the USA and India

Salary figures can look precise while describing different jobs, experience levels, and parts of a compensation package. Before comparing a number from India with one from the USA, check what the source measured and whether the roles are actually comparable.

This guide is a research checklist, not a salary survey. It does not predict a personal offer or promise a particular career outcome.

## Start with the work behind the title

“AI engineer” can describe work in model-powered application development, machine-learning systems, data pipelines, evaluation, or research. Two jobs with the same title may have different responsibilities. Start by comparing the job descriptions: What will the person build? What systems will they own? What experience does the employer require?

Compare roles at a similar level and scope. Years on a résumé alone do not determine what work a person can take on or where an offer will land in a published band.

## Check each guide’s date and method

At the time of this review, Robert Half publishes a 2027 technology salary guide for US roles. Its benchmarks are starting salary projections, with ranges and city-specific options; they are not a universal average for every AI engineer. Its own notes explain that skills, certifications, industry, company size, revenue, and location can change pay.

For India, Michael Page’s public Salary Guide page offers its 2026 guide through a sign-up flow. TeamLease Digital’s FY2026–27 primer discusses digital skills and salary trends. These publications describe different periods and use different approaches. Do not combine their figures into a direct country ranking without checking each methodology and role definition.

When recording a source, capture the publication date, the period covered, geography, role definition, experience band, sample or data method, and whether the amount is base salary or total compensation. If those details are missing, label the source as limited evidence.

## Compare the complete offer

Put the following items on separate lines before deciding whether two offers are comparable:

- Base salary and pay frequency.
- Guaranteed and performance-based bonus.
- Equity value, vesting schedule, and liquidity conditions.
- Health coverage, retirement benefits, leave, and other benefits.
- Taxes, local living costs, relocation, and work authorization.
- Role level, responsibilities, working hours, and review cycle.

Currency conversion can help with arithmetic. It does not account for taxes, benefits, living costs, employment terms, or the fact that the salary guides may measure different kinds of pay.

## Use salary research to improve your job search

Choose the role you are actually targeting, then review several current job descriptions for it. List the skills that recur and choose one gap to address. A small project can help demonstrate a skill if it includes reproducible setup steps, example inputs and outputs, checks, and clear limitations.

When discussing an offer, ask how the role is levelled and request a written breakdown of compensation. Keep private offer details and other people’s pay private; an individual result is not a market sample.

## What this comparison cannot tell you

No salary guide can guarantee a particular offer or hiring result. The available data changes, and guides can use different definitions and collection methods. Recheck the sources when making a current decision and write down your assumptions instead of presenting one converted figure as a definitive answer.
